# Football Dynasty fictional identity policy

Football Dynasty is designed to feel like a real football world without depending on licensed player, club, competition, crest, sponsor or kit identities.

## Clubs

- Geographic place names may be used to ground clubs in believable football regions.
- Club identities are original aliases such as `Manchester Red`, `Merseyside Red`, `Madrid White`, `Munich Red` and similar fictional names.
- Official club names, official crests, sponsors and exact kit artwork are not part of the launch database.
- The game may use original colour palettes and procedural badges, but they should not reproduce an official badge or a complete protected trade dress.

## Players and staff

- The launch database contains procedurally generated fictional people.
- The generator uses country-specific first-name and surname pools, then builds unique combinations deterministically from the career seed.
- Real-player rosters are not recreated by changing one letter or by pairing near-identical names with matching clubs, ages, positions and attributes.
- Generated people are explicitly marked with `fictional_identity = true` in the world model.

## Competitions

- Competition mechanics can mirror familiar football structures: league sizes, double round robins, promotion/relegation, promotion play-offs, relegation play-offs, domestic cups, transfer windows and registration rules.
- Launch branding uses descriptive fictional competition names rather than relying on official sponsor or league branding.

## Modding

The game engine can remain data-driven so players may create their own private databases and graphics packs. Official releases should ship only the fictional database and original/procedural artwork unless separate rights have been obtained.
