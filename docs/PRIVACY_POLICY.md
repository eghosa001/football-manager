# Football Dynasty Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 14 September 2026**

Football Dynasty is a football-management game whose core career simulation stores saves, settings, and custom database data locally on the player's device. A user account is not required to play the core game.

## Local game data

Football Dynasty stores career saves, game settings, and any custom database or mod data you create locally on your device. This data is used to provide game functionality. Android backup is disabled for the current build, so uninstalling the application may remove locally stored game data.

## Google Play Billing

The Android Google Play version may use Google Play Billing to sell the optional one-time **Football Dynasty Premium** entitlement and to restore purchases. Google may process information necessary to complete, verify, acknowledge, and restore transactions, including Google Play account, transaction, device, and diagnostic information. Football Dynasty receives purchase-state information required to determine whether Premium is owned. The game stores a local Premium entitlement flag after Google Play confirms ownership so the entitlement can continue to work while offline.

Football Dynasty does not store payment-card details. Payment processing is handled by Google Play.

## Optional rewarded advertising

Free players may voluntarily request rewarded advertisements. Rewarded advertising is not required to continue a career, play matches, advance time, use tactics, make transfers, save, or load a game. Football Dynasty does not use forced interstitial ads or app-open ads in the monetization design.

The Android build may use Google Mobile Ads/AdMob for rewarded ads. Depending on Google SDK configuration, consent choices, region, and advertising settings, Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location inferred by the advertising platform, ad interaction information, consent state, and technical/diagnostic information for ad delivery, fraud prevention, measurement, and related advertising purposes.

Where required, consent choices must be collected through the advertising SDK's supported consent flow before personalized advertising is used.

## Internet access

The Android build may request Internet access for Google Play Billing and optional rewarded advertising. Core locally stored career gameplay does not depend on a permanent Internet connection.

## Premium

Football Dynasty Premium is intended as a non-consumable, one-time entitlement. It does not sell match victories, player attributes, transfer outcomes, or other competitive results. Premium may remove rewarded-ad prompts and unlock supporter/customisation conveniences while preserving the complete core management experience for free players.

## Data sharing and sale

Football Dynasty does not sell locally stored career data. Data processed by Google Play Billing or Google Mobile Ads is governed by the applicable Google services and policies. The Google Play Data Safety declaration for each release must match the exact SDK versions and configuration used by that release.

## Children and target audience

The developer must configure the Google Play target-audience declaration and advertising child-directed/age-of-consent settings consistently with the intended audience before production release. Advertising must not be enabled with an audience configuration that conflicts with Google Play Families or advertising requirements.

## Changes to this policy

This policy must be reviewed whenever advertising, billing, analytics, cloud saves, online accounts, telemetry, crash reporting, or another network service is added or materially changed.

## Contact

Support and privacy contact details must match the developer contact information published on the Football Dynasty Google Play store listing.
