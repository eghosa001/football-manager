# Football Dynasty Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 14 September 2026**

Football Dynasty is designed to function offline. The current Google Play release does not require a user account, does not request Internet access, and does not send gameplay data or personal information to the developer.

## Data stored on your device

Football Dynasty stores career saves, game settings, and any custom database or mod data you create locally on your device. This information is used only to provide game functionality on that device.

## Data collection and sharing

The current release does not include analytics, advertising SDKs, cloud saves, online accounts, social login, telemetry, crash-reporting SDKs, or third-party tracking. The current Android release does not request Internet permission. No personal or gameplay data is collected by the developer or shared with third parties by the game.

## Device backup and deletion

Android backup is disabled for the current release. Uninstalling the application may therefore permanently remove locally stored Football Dynasty data. Players should treat local saves as device-local data unless a future release explicitly adds an export or cloud-backup feature.

## Children

Football Dynasty does not knowingly collect personal data from children or other users because the current release does not collect user data.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version adds online services, advertising, analytics, cloud saves, purchases, accounts, telemetry, or any other data collection, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration must be updated before that version is released.

## Contact

Support and privacy contact details must match the developer contact information published on the Football Dynasty Google Play store listing.
